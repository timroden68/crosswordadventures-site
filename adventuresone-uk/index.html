Colour certificates

There are six certificates to earn in the book. Type a name, then download the certificate and print it in colour. They are A4, the same size as the book. Or download a blank one and write the name by hand.

The name stays on this device. It's added to the certificate right here in your browser and isn't sent anywhere.

Bonus puzzles

Six free puzzles to print, one from each level of the book, from Warm-Up to Champion. They are A4, with British spelling. The answers are on the last page.

Download the bonus puzzles (PDF)

Solve in the app

Anyone can play free sample puzzles in the Crossword Adventures app on a phone, tablet or computer. With the book, you can also unlock all 100 of its puzzles there by answering a question from the book.
